**Goal:** give the `fused` suggestion the two-line window the repair corpus already measures,
then put the period widening in front of the bar v0.9d preregistered —

scores it against calibration as tuning feedback that decides nothing,

No class ships from this plan's executing session;

pytest for the gate;
the corpus instrument under `tests/corpus/` extended where named, unchanged in its bar;

## Global Constraints

- The core stays one stdlib-only Python 3.9+ file: `scripts/check_linefeeds.py` (ADR-0004).
- Precision over recall: a withheld suggestion is acceptable, a wrong delivered one is a defect.
- No path writes a user's file; a suggestion is delivered text, never an edit (umbrella, ADR-0007).
- No class is admissible on the calibration side (`repair_admission.admissible_from`).
- Every Markdown file touched passes `python3 scripts/check_linefeeds.py --file <files>`.
- Full gate before any commit: `python3 -m pytest tests/ -q` with bun on `PATH`.
- The candidate universe bound is not widened: `MAX_CUTS` stays 2 and `CUT_PUNCTUATION` stays as it is
  (measured 2026-08-20: a wider bound costs a near-full re-round and moves the headline by at most two units).
- Accepted corpus evidence is never edited:
  acceptable sets, passes, strata, and inclusion probabilities are byte-identical before and after every task,
  and a migration that would touch them refuses instead.

## The session boundary, stated once and early

The holdout ledger enforces ordering: freeze before draw, one freeze per round.
It cannot enforce who labels.
A session that tuned the predicate has read the material its labels would score,
so its labels measure memory rather than generalization.

Therefore:

- Tasks 1–8 belong to any executing session, this one included.
  They build, tune against calibration, build the round-4 machinery against fixtures,
  and prepare the handoff.
  **The executing session stops at the end of Task 8.**
- Task 9 belongs to a session that did not tune.
  It selects round 4's sources, writes the freeze, draws, labels, seals,
  scores, and records the admission decision.
- The executing session does not select, name, or browse candidate round-4 sources.
  Source selection is bound into the freeze (ADR-0024), and the freeze is Task 9's to write.

## Design decisions this plan locks in

**The window trigger is the detector's own `wrap` pairing.**
The suggestion absorbs the line below on the detector's own pairing only:
a `wrap` anchored on the fused finding's line,
and not suppressed there.
No second definition of "the sentence continues" may exist;
the wrap finder and the suggestion consult one shared predicate,
so the two cannot drift.
A suppressed `wrap` means the user blessed the break,
so the suggestion falls back to the one-line shape rather than overriding a directive.

**The absorbed shape stays two lines.**
Rejoin, then one split at the fused boundary:
`[leader + p1, leader + p2 + " " + below_prose + below_tail]`.
The anchor's whitespace-only tail, its terminator, and the break are replaced by one joining space,
which is the same normalization `repair_window` applies to the window's prose.
Nothing else moves, and no word changes.
On the corpus's joined window this is a one-cut break vector at an enumerated position,
so it is always expressible inside the frozen universe.

**Below-line safety is a set of named withholding classes, computed only for paired windows.**
Extending the taxonomy is allowed exactly once, here, before anything is frozen against it;
ADR-0024 binds the taxonomy into round 4's freeze,
and the population is re-measured under the extended taxonomy before the handoff.
The terminator the join consumes is part of that safety:
the absorbed form exists only where the anchor's terminator is a bare LF,
tested against `record["terminator"]` rather than against a raw line
that never carries its terminator.

**A suggestion says what it replaces.**
`suggestion` gains a `replaces` field, always present, counting raw lines from `line`.
The meaning of `lines` changes from "replaces the anchor line" to "replaces `replaces` lines",
which is a breaking change to the document,
so `DIAGNOSTIC_SCHEMA_VERSION` goes to 2, every consumer and pin moves with it,
and the spec says why.

**The candidate is a committed constant, and the freeze binds it.**
Two frozensets sit side by side in `scripts/check_linefeeds.py`:
`ADMITTED = frozenset()`, the shipped set, empty until an admission ADR flips it;
and `CANDIDATE_ADMITTED = frozenset({"terminator_period"})`, the one candidate under test.
`_fused_suggestion` takes `admitted`, and exactly two values may ever reach it:
those two constants.
The scorer selects by name — `shipped` or `candidate` — and accepts no free class list,
so the predicate digest the freeze already records binds the algorithm
and the candidate in one hash,
and nothing chosen after the prose is read can redefine what round 4 scores.
The round-4 freeze additionally binds the scoring code itself
(`score.py` and the harness it leans on),
so the transformation from admitted set to normalized repair cannot move between the freeze and the seal.

**One composition algorithm, `replaces`-aware, shared by every scorer.**
`compose(window, lines)` is replaced by `composed(window, suggestion)`,
which consumes the whole suggestion object:
`replaces == 2` uses the suggested lines alone,
`replaces == 1` on a two-line window appends the untouched lower raw line,
and anything else — 0, 3, or `replaces == 2` on a one-line window — refuses rather than scores.
`promote.py` and `score.py` both call it,
so `same_algorithm` is a checked fact rather than a naming convention.

**A sealed round is opened once, and that one open scores both sides.**
`score.py --predicate` exists for calibration samples only.
A sealed bundle is scored by one atomic command that authenticates,
records the spend before any decrypted plaintext can escape,
computes `candidate` and `shipped` in one process through `composed` and `normalize_repair`,
and appends one paired evaluation — or a failed-evaluation state,
so a scorer crash cannot make the bundle reusable (ADR-0008).
The seal's own integrity check never calls the spend-capable open path.

**Round-4 sources are validated before they are frozen, and the binding is round-aware.**
One canonical validator, `repair_round_sources(document, round)`,
is consulted by `freeze.py --repair`, `draw.py --round`, and the repair seal alike:
exactly one source per composition in `COMPOSITIONS`,
a qualification record on every one,
and no id or repository URL reused from the twelve declared sources.
`repair_round_bindings` becomes `repair_round_bindings(document, round)`,
and its `sources` digest is computed from the exact canonical object that validator returns,
so freeze, draw, and seal hash the same round-specific selection or refuse together.
The freeze refuses before appending a ledger line when validation fails,
because a freeze is one per round and cannot be retried into validity.

**An admission is a validated record, not a file's presence.**
`repair_admission_result` is a versioned machine-readable manifest record:
it names the exact admitted set, the round, the pre-draw freeze id,
the evaluation digest, the candidate predicate digest as frozen,
every activated stratum's counts and interval,
every zero-tolerance count, the outcome, and the decision ADR.
A harness validator checks it against the append-only ledger,
and the two-state precision guard derives its answer from that validated record —
mutating any field of it turns the guard red.

**Baselines are provenance-preserving snapshots, never edits.**
The promoted `baseline_suggestion` records stay exactly as they are,
bound to the digest of the predicate that produced them.
The shape predicate's baselines live in a separate top-level manifest section,
`repair_baselines_v2`, keyed by repair unit id and carrying its own summary and validator,
written by one deterministic command that repins the lock in the same commit.
The existing `repairs` entries and `reporting.repair_measured` stay byte-identical,
and the migration refuses duplicate, missing, or unknown unit ids before writing.
Accepted evidence — acceptable sets, passes, strata, inclusion probabilities — never changes.
